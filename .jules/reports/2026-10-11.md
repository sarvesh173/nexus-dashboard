# Sentinel Audit Report — 2026-10-11

## Findings Summary

### [med][reliability] Optional third-party packages (`psutil`, `yaml`, `requests`) cause import-time `ModuleNotFoundError`

- **File**: `server.py:3-10`, `model_health.py:26`, `run_sync.py:27-28`
- **Type**: Reliability / Error Handling
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Confidence**: High
- **Description**: Top-level imports for `psutil`, `yaml`, and `requests` were not guarded with `try...except ImportError`. Environments without these packages crashed upon importing backend modules rather than safely falling back or reporting degraded capabilities.
- **Fix**: Wrapped imports in `try...except ImportError` and added safe fallbacks across telemetry and sync functions.
