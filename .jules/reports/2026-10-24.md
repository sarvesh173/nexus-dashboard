# Sentinel Audit Report - 2026-10-24

### [low][bug] Normalize ISO 8601 timestamps in `build_console_log_entries` sorting

**Summary**
In `server.py`, `build_console_log_entries()` merges Hermes log tail records with OmniRoute call logs and sorts them chronologically. The `sort_key` function attempted to parse timestamp strings using `time.strptime(e['timestamp'][:19], '%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S')`. While Hermes log timestamps use spaces (`2026-10-07 12:00:00`), OmniRoute call log timestamps are ISO 8601 strings containing a `'T'` (`2026-10-07T12:00:00.000Z`).

**Evidence**
- `server.py:634`
- Repro: `time.strptime('2026-10-07T12:00:00', '%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S')` raises `ValueError: time data '2026-10-07T12:00:00' does not match format '%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S'`.

**Impact**
Any call log entry with a `'T'` separator caused `time.strptime` to raise `ValueError`, catching the exception and returning `(1, 0)`. As a result, OmniRoute call logs were failing to sort and were sorted out of order at the bottom of the console feed.

**Suggested Fix / Resolution**
Normalize the timestamp string by replacing `'T'` with `' '` prior to parsing in `sort_key`:
`ts_str = str(e.get('timestamp') or '')[:19].replace('T', ' ')`

**Severity**: Low
